- [ ] **Bing Places** (bingplaces.com): import from Google Business Profile.

- [ ] **The Knot** and **WeddingWire**: free vendor listings with a website link.
- [ ] **Zola** vendor directory.
- [ ] South Asian wedding directories: **ShaadiBaraati**, **Maharani Weddings**, **WedMeGood** (US).
- [ ] **Yelp** business page.
- [ ] **Farmington Hills / Greater Farmington Area Chamber of Commerce** member directory.
- [ ] **Nextdoor** business page.
- [ ] **Vendor partners:** ask venues, Miles To Go Photos, caterers and florists you work with to list you on their "preferred vendors" page with a link, and credit them back.

## Ongoing
