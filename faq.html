Our Services

What does Celebrate with Bhavya's do?

Celebrate with Bhavya's is a full-service event planning and event decor company based in Farmington Hills, Michigan. We plan and design Indian weddings, receptions, engagements, sangeet, mehndi, haldi and pooja ceremonies, graduation parties, birthdays, housewarmings, baby showers and corporate events across metro Detroit and Michigan.

Do you offer both event planning and decor?

Yes. We are an event planner and an event decorator under one roof. You can book full event planning, decor and design, or both together for a true end-to-end experience with one team, one vision and one point of contact from the first idea to the final toast.

Can I book decor only, or planning only?

Absolutely. Many clients book us just for decor (stages, mandaps, backdrops, florals and tablescapes), while others book full planning, partial planning or day-of coordination. Choosing both gives you the most seamless, stress-free celebration.

What types of events do you plan and decorate?

Weddings, receptions, engagements, sangeet, mehndi, haldi, pooja and religious ceremonies, dhoti and half-saree ceremonies, baby showers, birthday parties, graduation parties, housewarmings, anniversaries and corporate or social events, big or small.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Farmington Hills, Michigan, and plan and decorate events throughout metro Detroit, including Novi, Troy, Detroit and Ann Arbor, as well as across Michigan. Reach out with your venue and date and we'll confirm availability.

Decor & Design

What kind of decor do you provide?

We design and install mandaps, wedding stages and backdrops, floral arches, entrances and aisles, sweetheart tables, centerpieces and tablescapes, welcome signs, photo spots, lighting and more, all styled to match your theme and color palette.

Where does your decor come from?

Much of our decor is from our own collection, with new pieces brought straight from India. That means authentic Indian wedding decor, from brass lamps and gold accents to carved stages and traditional details, alongside modern and luxury styles.

Do you specialize in Indian and South Asian wedding decor?

Yes. We are an Indian wedding decorator and planner who understands every tradition, from the pooja and haldi to the baraat, Kashi Yatra, wedding ceremony and reception, and we design a unique look for each day of a multi-day celebration.

Can you match a specific theme or color palette?

Yes. Every event is custom designed. Share your inspiration photos, colors and ideas, and we'll create a cohesive design for your stage, florals, tables and entrance that fits your venue and budget.

Do you handle decor setup and teardown?

Yes. Our team delivers, sets up and styles everything before your guests arrive, and takes care of teardown afterward, so you and your family can simply enjoy the celebration.

Do you decorate at homes, backyards and outdoor venues?

Yes. We decorate banquet halls, hotels, golf courses, gardens, backyards and homes, from intimate haldi and pooja setups in a living room to outdoor garden mandaps and grand ballroom receptions.

Event Planning

What is included in full event planning?

Full planning can include event conceptualization, venue selection, budget management, catering and beverages, entertainment, photography and video, audiovisual and lighting, wedding cards and invitations, makeup, hair and saree draping, transportation, event staffing and on-site event management.

Do you plan multi-day Indian weddings?

Yes. We plan and style every event of a multi-day wedding, including the haldi, mehndi, sangeet, pooja, baraat, wedding ceremony and reception, with a timeline that keeps every day running smoothly.

Do you offer partial planning or day-of coordination?

Yes. If you have already started planning, we can step in with partial planning or day-of coordination to manage vendors, timelines and the details on the day so nothing is missed.

Will you coordinate with our vendors?

Yes. We coordinate with your venue, caterer, photographer, DJ and other vendors, or help you choose them, so everyone is working from the same plan.

Booking & Pricing

How much do your services cost?

Every event is quoted individually based on your guest count, venue, decor design and the services you choose. Request a free quote and we'll put together a package that fits your vision and budget.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as you can, especially for weddings and dates in peak season, since popular dates fill quickly. Even if your date is close, contact us and we'll do our best to make it happen.

Do you take on small and intimate events?

Yes. Whether it's a 30-guest pooja at home or a 500-guest wedding reception, every celebration gets the same care and attention to detail.
