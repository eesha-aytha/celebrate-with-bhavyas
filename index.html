Our Story

A shared passion, turned into unforgettable experiences.

We both shared a love for planning events, organizing celebrations of our own and for friends. Along the way we realized we could turn that passion into a business, and saw a real need for high-quality event planning with a personal touch.

Whether it's a big or small event (birthdays, weddings, receptions, engagements, graduations or social gatherings), we work closely with you to understand your vision and handle every detail with precision and care.
