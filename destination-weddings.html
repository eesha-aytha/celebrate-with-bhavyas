Destination Weddings

Wherever you say "I do."

Destination wedding planning and decor from Michigan. From a lakeside weekend up north to a beach, resort or international celebration, we plan, design and decorate your destination wedding, then travel to bring it to life.

From Michigan to anywhere

The same personal touch, wherever you celebrate.

Planning a wedding away from home can feel overwhelming. We take that weight off your shoulders. Our team handles the planning and coordination from Michigan, works with trusted vendors at your destination, and travels to set up every detail of your decor and run the celebration on the day.

Whether it's an intimate elopement by the water, a resort or beach wedding weekend, or a multi-day Indian, fusion or multicultural celebration, you get one destination wedding planner and decorator, start to finish.

Destination wedding questions

Where do you plan destination weddings?

Anywhere you'd like to celebrate: lakeside and resort towns up north in Michigan, other states across the US, and destinations abroad. Tell us where you're dreaming of and we'll plan around it.

How far in advance should we book a destination wedding?

As early as you can, ideally once you have a location and a date in mind. Destination weddings involve travel, venue rules and local vendors, so more time gives you more options.

Do you bring your own decor to the destination?

Yes. We can bring signature pieces from our own collection, including decor brought straight from India, and combine them with florals and rentals sourced near your venue so your design travels well.

How are travel costs handled?

Every destination wedding is quoted individually. We'll walk you through travel and setup costs in your custom quote so there are no surprises.

Can you plan a multi-day destination celebration?

Absolutely. From a welcome dinner, mehndi or sangeet to the ceremony, reception and farewell brunch, we plan and style every event of your destination weekend.
