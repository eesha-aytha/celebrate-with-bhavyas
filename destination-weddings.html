Destination Wedding Planners

The same personal touch, wherever you celebrate.

Planning a wedding away from home can feel overwhelming. We take that weight off your shoulders. Our team plans and coordinates everything from Michigan, partners with trusted vendors at your destination, and travels to set up every detail of your decor and run the celebration.

Whether it’s an intimate elopement by the water, a beach or resort weekend, or a multi-day Indian, fusion or multicultural celebration, you get one destination wedding planner and decorator, start to finish.

A Sample Weekend

Your celebration, day by day.

Every wedding is different. Here’s how a four-day destination weekend can come together.

  1. Thursday

    Welcome

    Guests arrive to welcome bags and a relaxed welcome dinner.

  2. Friday

    Mehndi & Sangeet

    A colorful day of henna, music and dancing under the stars.

  3. Saturday

    Ceremony & Reception

    Haldi in the morning, vows at sunset, a reception to remember.

  4. Sunday

    Farewell

    A farewell brunch before everyone heads home with full hearts.
